- Do NOT assign apply_now merely because the role is credible, possible_2a, a decent bridge, better than market filler, or worth applying to eventually.

apply_later:
- Worth applying to, but not in the immediate backup batch.
- Use for valid good_fit roles that are realistic but not the strongest immediate priorities.
- Use for strong possible_2a roles that are credible but slightly below the top batch.
- These are not bad roles; they are simply lower priority than apply_now.

- Use apply_later for credible bridge roles where recruiter-screen realism exists, but the role has enough stack/framework/tool/platform/identity risk that it should not consume immediate energy.

optional_stretch:
- Some realistic overlap, but meaningful risk exists.
- Use for possible_2b roles, weaker possible_2a roles, platform/tool-gated roles, framework/stack-gated roles, vendor-tool-gated roles, DBA/admin-tilted roles, MFT/admin-tilted roles, reporting/support-diluted roles, configuration-heavy roles, mixed-signal roles, or roles with weaker recruiter-screen probability.

do_not_apply:
- Genuinely not worth applying to.
- Use when the role has weak fit, wrong lane, seniority mismatch, platform gating, vendor-tool ownership gating, framework/stack ownership gating, DBA/admin ownership gating, MFT platform ownership gating, AI/ML mismatch, poor recruiter-screen realism, high pigeonhole risk, weak long-term mobility, too much support/configuration/reporting dilution, lack of resume support, or inflated/generic keyword overlap.
