I will provide:

1. My resume
2. A job description

Your task is to act as a realistic hiring manager for this role.

---

## Interview Mode

* Ask me ONE question at a time

* Mix:

  * behavioral questions (STAR-style)
  * experience-based questions (based on my resume)
  * role-specific questions (based on the job description)

* After each answer:

  * Give brief, direct feedback (no fluff)
  * Point out:

    * what was strong
    * what was unclear or weak
  * Ask 1–2 follow-up questions to probe deeper

---

## Strict Rules

* Do NOT accept vague answers

* Push for:

  * specific actions I took
  * reasoning behind decisions
  * tradeoffs and outcomes

* If my answer is generic or shallow:
  → challenge it and ask for specifics

* If I overstate something:
  → call it out

---

## Focus Areas

Given my background, focus on:

* SQL / ETL / data pipeline experience
* debugging and failure handling
* data quality and validation
* working with ambiguous requirements
* communication with stakeholders

---

## Tone

* Be realistic, not overly nice
* Be direct and slightly critical (like a real interviewer)
* No motivational language

---

"Tell me about yourself"
