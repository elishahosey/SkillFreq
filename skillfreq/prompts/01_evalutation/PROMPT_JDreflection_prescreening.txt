Absolutely — add this as a **JD + My Thoughts prescreening prompt**.

```text
I will provide:

1. A job description
2. My own rough thoughts comparing my background to the JD

Your task is to turn my rough thoughts into a clear prescreening prep sheet.

Use my background:
- SQL/database experience
- ETL/data workflow experience
- software integration
- REST API/data exchange exposure
- production debugging
- validation/reconciliation
- Python automation/helper scripts
- experience in domain-specific/proprietary systems
- junior-to-mid level framing

Do NOT overstate my experience.
Do NOT invent tools I have not used.
Do NOT turn conceptual knowledge into hands-on experience.
Do NOT make me sound senior, architect-level, or platform-owner level.

For each major requirement in the JD, classify my alignment as:

- Strong / Direct
- Partial / Adjacent
- Weak / Ramp-Up
- Unknown / Need to Clarify

For each item, provide:
1. What I can honestly say
2. What I should avoid saying
3. How to phrase it in a recruiter screen
4. Whether it is a must-clarify risk

Also identify:
- Biggest stack gaps
- Strongest alignment points
- Possible “translation” opportunities where my experience maps to the role even if the exact tool differs
- Red-flag areas where I should not oversell
- Best question to ask the recruiter to clarify whether this is a fit

Finally, give me:

Fit Read:
[clean fit / possible fit / stretch fit / senior-leaning fit]

Prescreening Summary:
[5-7 bullet points I can review before the call]

Best Recruiter Screen Framing:
[A short paragraph I can say out loud]

Best Clarifying Question:
[One question that determines whether the role is realistic for me]

Here is the job description:
[PASTE JD HERE]

Here are my rough thoughts:
[PASTE MY THOUGHTS HERE]
```
